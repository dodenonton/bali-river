EVERYTHING YOU NEED TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Detailed answers about our Bali River Tubing, White Water Rafting, ATV Quad Bike, Cycling, and Volcano Trekking tours.

What is the difference between Bali River Tubing and White Water Rafting? +

River Tubing places you in your own individual, heavy-duty inflatable tube with protective handles along a narrower, crystal-clear volcanic canyon stream (Class I–II rapids). It is intimate, playful, and brings you right underneath canyon waterfalls. White Water Rafting takes place on the wider Ayung or Telaga Waja River in a shared raft (4 to 6 guests plus our river captain) where you paddle together through larger Class II–IV waves over 12 to 16 kilometers.

Do I need prior experience or swimming skills for Tubing, Rafting, or ATV? +

No prior experience or swimming ability is required! All guests wear high-buoyancy, CE-certified life jackets (PFDs) and helmets on the river, and our guides stay within arm's reach throughout the canyon. For the ATV Quad Bike tour, our 250cc bikes are fully automatic (thumb throttle + hand brakes) and begin with a practice circuit before entering the jungle trail.

What should I bring and wear for my adventure tour? +

We recommend wearing comfortable swimwear or quick-dry activewear, river sandals or sneakers (we provide clean rubber boots for all ATV riders), reef-safe sunscreen, and a full change of dry clothes for after your heated shower. Waterproof phone pouches, dry bags, fresh towels, soap, and lockers are provided free of charge at our Ubud basecamp.

Can we combine River Tubing or Rafting with ATV, Cycling, or Trekking in one day? +

Absolutely! Our Combo Packages are our most popular bookings. Because our Secret Canyon Tubing, Ayung Rafting, and Gorilla Cave ATV basecamps are located within 10 minutes of each other in Payangan/Ubud, you spend your day adventuring rather than sitting in traffic—and save up to 25% compared to booking separately.

What happens if it rains in Ubud on the day of our tour? +

Tropical rain actually makes River Tubing, White Water Rafting, and Muddy Jungle ATV even more exciting! We operate rain or shine as long as water levels meet our strict safety sensors. In the rare event that heavy mountain rain causes our safety officers to pause river operations, we offer a free reschedule or a 100% full refund.
