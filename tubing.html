SAFETY & PREPARATION

What to Expect on River Tubing

Is River Tubing safe for non-swimmers and children? +

Yes! Every guest is outfitted with a certified buoyancy life vest and safety helmet. River guides flank the stream at key intervals and assist through every gentle rapid. Children aged 6 and above can participate safely.

What is the difference between Tubing and Rafting? +

Tubing uses an individual circular tube in narrower, clearer canyon springs, giving you a quiet, immersive solo float. Rafting uses a large 6-person boat on the wider Ayung River where you paddle together as a crew.
